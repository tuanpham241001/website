Cohomology of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras

This is a reading group on cohomology of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras, following the book of the same name by D. B. Fuks.

Meetings take place on Tuesdays 15:00–16:00 in JCMB. If you want to join the mailing list, or would like to volunteer to give a talk, please email me.
