Ideals of homomorphic images of the enveloping algebra of the Witt algebra

arXiv preprint · 2026

arXiv

We make significant progress to the open conjecture that the enveloping algebra of the Witt algebra \(\mathrm{U}(W)\) satisfies the ascending chain condition (ACC) on two-sided ideals. We show that the images of the orbit homomorphisms, of every Gelfand-Kirillov dimension, satisfy ACC on ideals. These maps were the key ingredient in lifting the Dixmier map to the Witt and Virasoro algebras in earlier work.

We then classify the prime and primitive spectra of these images. The images approximate \(U(W)\) more closely as their GK-dimension grows, so this classification sheds new light on its ideal structure. We conclude with applications to the Dixmier map for \(W\).

On the boundary Carrollian conformal algebra

with Lucas Buzaglo, Xiao He, Haijun Tan, Girish S. Vishwa and Kaiming Zhao · Letters in Mathematical Physics · 2026

arXivjournal

We commence the systematic study of the boundary Carrollian conformal algebra (BCCA), a Lie algebra first discovered in a physical context in this paper. The BCCA is an intriguing object from both physical and mathematical perspectives, since it is an infinite-dimensional Lie algebra which is filtered but not graded. In this paper, we establish some structural Lie algebraic properties and construct and study Whittaker modules for this Lie algebra.

The kernel and image of orbit homomorphisms for the Witt algebra

with James Timmins · arXiv preprint · 2025

arXiv

We study the two-sided ideals of the enveloping algebra \(\mathrm{U}(W)\) of the Witt algebra through the "orbit homomorphisms" \(\Psi_n : \mathrm{U}(W) \to T_n\), an infinite family of maps to noncommutative Noetherian algebras. These maps lift primitive ideals of finite-dimensional solvable Lie algebras to \(\mathrm{U}(W)\), and are central to the orbit method for the Witt algebra. We show that \(\ker \Psi_n\) is generated as a one-sided ideal by an infinite set of differentiators, but as a two-sided ideal by any single one of them. As a consequence, we describe explicitly the primitive and semi-primitive ideals corresponding to one-point local functions. We also show that the image \(B_n\) of \(\Psi_n\) is non-Noetherian, while being birational to \(T_n\). On the other hand, the degree-zero subring of \(B_n\) is left and right Noetherian, and we conjecture that the same is true of the degree-zero subring of \(\mathrm{U}(W)\).

The orbit method for the Virasoro algebra

arXiv preprint · 2025

arXiv

We construct representations of the Witt and Virasoro algebras following the orbit method, and prove that their annihilators are completely prime primitive ideals. We construct a well-defined Dixmier map from the Poisson primitive spectrum of the symmetric algebra of Vir to the primitive spectrum of the universal enveloping algebra of Vir.

Our method involves new ring homomorphisms from \(\mathrm{U}(W)\) to the tensor product of a localized Weyl algebra and the enveloping algebra of a finite-dimensional solvable subquotient of \(W\). We further show that the kernels of these homomorphisms are intersections of the primitive ideals constructed from natural subsets of \(W^*\).

What do graph neural networks learn? Insights from tropical geometry

with Vikas Garg · NeurIPS · 2024

NeurIPS 2024

We show that message-passing GNNs with ReLU activations learn exactly the continuous piecewise linear functions (tropical rational signomial maps), the same class as feedforward networks. We then elucidate the role of the choice of aggregation and update functions, and derive the first general upper and lower bounds on the geometric complexity (i.e., the number of linear regions), establishing new results for popular architectures such as GraphSAGE and GIN. We also introduce and theoretically analyze several new architectures to illuminate the relative merits of the feedforward and the message passing layers, and the tradeoffs involving depth and number of trainable parameters. Finally, we also characterize the decision boundary for node and graph classification tasks.

Two-sided ideals of the enveloping algebras of the Virasoro and related Lie algebras

University of Edinburgh · supervised by Prof. Susan Sierra · 2026

DOI

In this thesis, following Dixmier's philosophy, we investigate primitive ideals and two-sided ideals of the enveloping algebras of these infinite-dimensional Lie algebras, and give applications to their representation theory. We construct a Dixmier map from the Poisson primitive spectrum of the symmetric algebra of the Witt algebra to the primitive spectrum of the enveloping algebra of the Witt algebra, successfully extending Kirillov's orbit method for finite-dimensional solvable Lie algebras to our countable-dimensional setting.

Our method involves new ring homomorphisms, called “orbit homomorphisms”, from \( U(\mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{C}[t])) \) to the tensor product of the first Weyl algebra \(A_1\) and the enveloping algebra of a finite-dimensional solvable subquotient \(\mathfrak{g}_n\) of \(\mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{C}[t])\). We prove that the kernel of an orbit homomorphism is infinitely generated as a one-sided ideal, whilst being principal as a two-sided ideal. we show that the images of these orbit homomorphisms all satisfy the ascending chain condition on two-sided ideals, making significant progress on the conjecture of Sierra and Walton that \( U(\mathrm{Der}(\mathbb{C}[t])) \) satisfies the ascending chain condition on two-sided ideals.
