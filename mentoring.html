Quantum invariants arising from \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\) are q-holonomic

Summer 2026 · Count Me In programme, AGQ CDT

In summer 2026, Jenny Brown and I supervised three new undergraduated students, Sarah-Sonia Balan (Manchester), Eve Ryan (Edinburgh) and Martha Watson (Oxford) to continue exploring q-holonomic properties obtained from typical representations of \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\).

Using the root system and Cartan matrix of \(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1)\), they obtained an explicit presentation of \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\). They then found the explicit decomposition of \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\) typical representations into direct sum of 8 \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3))\) representations. They constructed a canonical basis and worked out explicit actions of the odd generators between these subrepresentations.

Quantum group \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\) and its typical representations

Summer 2025 · Count Me In programme, AGQ CDT

In summer 2025, Jenny Brown and I supervised three students, Faith Hurst (St. Andrews), Eleanor Milburn (Warwick), Christina Sieker (Edinburgh) to explore typical representations of the quantum group \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\).

They studied the Cartan subalgebra of \(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1)\) and calculated the corresponding root system and standard Cartan matrix. They worked out an explicit presentation of \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\) and compute conditions on the weight that are necessary for a highest weight \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\)-representation to be typical. Finally, they explored the use of two different subalgebras of \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\) in creating a direct sum decomposition of typical \(U_h(\mathfrak{sl}(3|1))\)-representations, and gave a conjecture about the structure of such a decomposition.
